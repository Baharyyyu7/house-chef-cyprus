# Global Food Platform Benchmark

**Purpose:** Identify reusable product principles from publicly visible behaviour only. This is not a content, media, data, HTML, or design-copying exercise. Observations were made on 3 August 2026; platform behaviour can change.

## Method and guardrails

- Examined public home, discovery, recipe, editorial, and account entry points where available.
- Stored only pattern-level findings and public entry URLs. No competitor recipe text, images, ratings, user comments, structured data, or datasets were copied.
- No login bypass, paywall bypass, private endpoint access, scraping, or automated import was used.
- Recommendations below are original House Chef Cyprus proposals, not reproductions.

## Platforms sampled

| Platform | Public pattern observed | Useful principle | Avoid copying |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Nefis Yemek Tarifleri](https://www.nefisyemektarifleri.com/) | Dense discovery around recipes, video, lists, ingredient-led matching, and community contribution | Make an immediate "what can I cook?" path visible | Contributor identity, recipe corpus, counts, wording, and community UI |
| [Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes) | Strong collection taxonomy combining time, occasion, seasonality, diet, and recent content | Treat collection landing pages as curated editorial destinations | Rating displays, branded labels, copy, and commercial gating patterns |
| [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/) | Recipe cards clearly surface time, save action, and real community signals | Keep repeat actions visible, but only show genuine signals | Reviews, ratings, contributor content, and numerical social proof |
| [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/) | Technique and food-science editorial authority sits beside recipes | Explain *why* only when sourcing and editorial review support it | Editorial text, tests, technical claims, and staff presentation |
| [Kitchen Stories](https://www.kitchenstories.com/en) | App-aware discovery with daily inspiration, articles, and clear content modes | Separate "cook now" from longer inspiration journeys | Layout, imagery, subscription presentation, or creator material |
| [Taste](https://www.taste.com.au/) | Seasonal and occasion-led merchandising mixes recipes and guides | Build an original seasonal calendar for Cyprus and Mediterranean context | Their categories, copy, and promotions |
| [EatingWell](https://www.eatingwell.com/) | Health-oriented discovery needs strong editorial and safety framing | Health claims require authoritative sourcing and review state | Nutritional and health content or claims |
| [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/) | Subscription-led planning and saved-recipe utility is a distinct product layer | Planning, saves, and lists have higher retention value than decorative features | Paywalled content, identity, visual language, and paid workflow |

## Cross-platform findings

1. **Discovery wins when intent is explicit.** Time, available ingredients, diet, meal type, and occasion need separate but combinable entry points.
2. **Recipes earn trust through operational detail.** Clear servings, times, ingredients, steps, source/editor metadata, and honest uncertainty beat decorative badges.
3. **Editorial and recipes should reinforce each other.** Guides should lead to relevant recipes; recipe details should offer genuinely related guides.
4. **Community value is real only with moderation.** Ratings, comments, and user media are not launch features without abuse controls and review queues.
5. **Real signals must remain real.** Popularity, ratings, saves, and editor picks need evidence; otherwise they should be absent.
6. **Mobile is the primary working surface.** Filter drawers, persistent applied-filter chips, readable ingredients, and low-friction save/plan actions matter more than desktop decoration.

## House Chef Cyprus differentiator

House Chef Cyprus should be an original, privacy-conscious Mediterranean home-cooking platform: verified public recipes; explicit confidence and allergen language; household and canonical measurements; ingredient/pantry matching; calm editorial guidance; and no invented social proof.
