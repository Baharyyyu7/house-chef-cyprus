# Blog and Editorial Benchmark

## Public pattern synthesis

Leading food platforms use guides to solve a cooking question, support a season or technique, and direct readers toward relevant recipes. Strong pages make author/editor context, update date, headings, related content, and media intent visible. Health, allergen, storage, and food-safety topics need an evidence trail rather than a persuasive tone.

## House Chef audit

`/blog` has search, a responsive card grid, pagination, and intentional text-only cards when approved media is absent. Detail pages include a canonical URL, author/editor presentation, media provenance where available, related recipes, and related guides. The visible gap is richer topic navigation, table of contents, review-date signalling, source display, and consistent editorial depth.

## Original direction

- Introduce editorial types: **guide**, **technique**, **ingredient note**, **seasonal plan**, and **safety explainer**.
- Require a purpose statement, 3 meaningful headings, reviewed-date field, source requirements, and linked recipe candidates before editorial approval.
- Permit an intentional text-only design; do not insert a decorative pseudo-photo.
- Publish high-risk guidance only after a named, authorised human review and source references are present.
