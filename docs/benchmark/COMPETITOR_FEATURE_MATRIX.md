## Implications

- House Chef already has the rarer foundation: household/canonical measurement, nutrition, allergen, dietary, pantry matching, data quality, and editorial controls.
- The visible gap is orchestration: discovery controls, collection architecture, higher-density but honest recipe cards, and a coherent relationship between recipes and guides.
- Community, ratings, popularity, and monetization remain **not approved for public display** until backed by real data, policy, moderation, and consent.
