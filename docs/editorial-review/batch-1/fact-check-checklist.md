# Batch 1 fact-check checklist

This checklist controls private editorial review. A checked item is not an
approval unless an authenticated human reviewer records it in the review
workflow.

## Recipe checks

- [ ] Reconcile title, ingredient identity, quantities, canonical units,
      household units, servings, preparation method, and yield.
- [ ] Test or otherwise document each recipe-specific method; generic steps do
      not qualify.
- [ ] Compute nutrition only from the final formulation and state that it is an
      estimate, not medical advice.
- [ ] Map only allergens supported by the final ingredients; distinguish
      `contains` from uncertain cross-contact information.
- [ ] For poultry, ground meat, storage, or reheating language, cite an
      authoritative source and avoid colour-only doneness claims.
- [ ] Do not label an untested culinary interpretation as authentic.
- [ ] Do not select, import, approve, or publish media until dish identity is
      reconciled and a human media reviewer approves its license/provenance.

## Blog source requirements

| Topic | Required authority | Editorial guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allergen labels | [FDA Food Allergies](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies) plus target-market rule | Scope the claim to the cited jurisdiction; no clinical guidance. |
| Estimated nutrition | [FDA Nutrition Facts Label education](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/how-understand-and-use-nutrition-facts-label) plus House Chef calculation method | Do not imply that an estimate is a diagnosis, label, or personalized target. |
| Leftovers and reheating | [USDA FSIS leftovers guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) | State the source jurisdiction and verify each time/temperature figure before publication. |
| Oven temperature | [FoodSafety.gov temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) | Use a thermometer context for meat/poultry; recipe time is not a safety guarantee. |
| Cyprus culinary context | At least one reputable Cyprus cultural/academic source per historical claim | Avoid one-size-fits-all authenticity statements. |

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No rewriting, scraping, or paraphrasing third-party recipe/blog copy.
- No copyrighted food image as a media source without a license and review.
- No fabricated source, approval, reviewer, nutrition, allergen, or safety
  claim.
- No automatic publish, verify, schedule, or public media action.
