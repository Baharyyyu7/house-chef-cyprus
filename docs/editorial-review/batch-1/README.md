# Content Completion Batch 1 — Private Editorial Packet

**Purpose:** prepare, but do not apply, the first human-review batch for ten
published recipes and five published blog posts. This packet is not a content
import, approval, publication, verification, or media-selection record.

## Scope and safety boundary

- No production database table was written while preparing this packet.
- No recipe or blog was changed, published, verified, or scheduled.
- No media was generated, imported, selected, or made public.
- `READY_FOR_EDITOR_REVIEW` means its review brief is complete; it is **not** a
  content, fact, nutrition, allergen, or media approval.
- Recipe titles and current catalog data were read from the local verified
  catalog only. No third-party recipe or blog text, photograph, or derivative
  rewrite is included.

## Why these items were selected

The selection is an editorial-priority hypothesis based on public visibility,
broad household use, Mediterranean/Cyprus relevance, and the local audit's
content/media gaps. It is not a traffic or search-volume claim. The ten recipe
records deliberately include identity/data mismatches that require an editor
before any content change. The five blogs include the four requested
high-risk subjects plus a Cyprus discovery article with evergreen intent.

## Editorial hand-off

1. An assigned human editor reconciles each recipe title, ingredients, method,
   serving size, and domestic/canonical measurements against lawful first-party
   notes or independently researched sources.
2. A qualified reviewer clears the health, allergen, storage, reheating, and
   temperature claims referenced in the checklist.
3. A separate media reviewer approves a licensed, dish-accurate candidate.
4. Only then may an authorized editor create a revision proposal. Human
   approval remains mandatory; this packet never changes publication state.
