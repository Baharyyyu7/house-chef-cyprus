# SEO Health Rules

Audit rules cover missing metadata, publish-gate violations, duplicate explicit metadata, canonical mismatch, thin-content review, missing related recipe links, missing public recipe images and missing image alt text. Findings are fingerprinted, idempotent and resolved when the evidence disappears.

Scores are transparent issue-penalty calculations: critical 20, error 10, warning 4 and info 1. A score is a remediation indicator only; it is never a domain-authority, ranking or quality certification.
