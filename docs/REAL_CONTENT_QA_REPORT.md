# Real Content QA Report

## Seed verification baseline

- 50 published, verified recipe records
- 50 active recipe provenance records
- 20 approved, published blog posts
- 20 blog posts with two or more verified recipe links
- no public blog body containing `lorem ipsum` or `özgün demo`
- all seeded blog posts carry a unique public hero-media record

## Explicit remaining editorial work

The catalogue does not claim to have 50 verified food photographs. Its current
media is an original, uniquely generated **branded fallback** per recipe, with
alt text and a replacement-required marker. Replacing all fallbacks requires an
approved/licensed image provider or original photography plus editorial review;
it must never be automated as a public publication action.

Recipe nutrition and allergen output retains its existing origin and confidence
fields. A value with calculated, editorial or estimated origin must not be
presented as laboratory-verified nutrition.
