# Media Fidelity Standard

Candidate matching is conservative: title and ingredient signals support a review queue, not an automatic publication decision. Outcomes are `exact_match`, `strong_match`, `acceptable_match`, `uncertain`, `mismatch` and `prohibited` with a 0–100 confidence value and saved reasons.

`uncertain`, `mismatch` and `prohibited` are never selectable automatically. `acceptable_match` remains human-review-only. Reviewers must check the dish, key ingredients, cuisine, preparation and presentation before selecting a candidate.
