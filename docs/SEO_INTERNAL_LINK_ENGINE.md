# SEO Internal Link Engine

The internal-link service suggests only already published and verified recipes for an eligible blog. It never creates links or alters public copy automatically. `seo:audit` flags a public, approved blog without a related recipe as a discovery opportunity; this is not proof of an SEO defect and needs editorial judgement.

Recipe detail suggestions are limited to visible recipes sharing the same category or cuisine. Links are server-rendered, descriptive and do not expose internal identifiers.
