## Publishing Guidelines

1. Each guest post must include a bio section linking to housechefcyprus.com
2. Anchor text distribution: 40% keyword-rich, 30% branded, 30% generic
3. All posts must be original content, minimum 1500 words
4. Include high-quality images with alt text containing target keywords
5. Internal linking: 2-3 internal links to housechefcyprus.com recipe pages
6. All posts must be published in both Turkish and English versions where applicable
