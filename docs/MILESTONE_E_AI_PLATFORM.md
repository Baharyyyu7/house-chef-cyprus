# Milestone E — AI Kitchen Production Platform

Milestone E introduces an opt-in, authenticated AI Kitchen layer with provider abstraction, response cache, rate limiting, safety controls, audit records, meal/shopping drafts, pantry/leftover support and admin observability. It preserves the authoritative recipe catalogue: AI cannot publish, verify or replace content.

Remote providers are configuration seams, not automatic integrations. Production enables them only after privacy, cost, timeout, fallback and human operational approval are complete.
