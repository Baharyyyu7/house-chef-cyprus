# House Chef Cyprus Feature Roadmap

## Phase A — benchmark and product definition (this package)

Deliver benchmark, gap analysis, information architecture, acceptance criteria, and implementation sequencing. **No production data change.**

## Phase B — homepage, navigation, and recipe discovery

- Original homepage refinement around useful, data-supported discovery.
- Taxonomy/route contract and mobile search/filter experience.
- No popularity, ratings, or editor picks unless underlying records support them.

## Phase C — recipe cards and detail utility

- One card/media contract, content-quality visibility rules, print, and full recipe-detail QA.
- Public approved-media gate and responsive media derivatives.

## Phase D — blog and editorial destination

- Editorial taxonomy, listing/detail improvements, source/review presentation, related content.
- High-risk topics remain unpublished without sources and human review.

## Phase E — quality engine and media workflow

- Consolidated publish-readiness, media lifecycle visibility, quality audits, and operational admin surfaces.

## Phase F — controlled published-content completion

- Human-reviewed batches only. No auto verification, auto publishing, competitor import, or synthetic approval.

## Phase G — retention utility

- Collections, saved filters, shopping-list and meal-plan reliability, preference/privacy controls.

## Phase H — admin and controlled automation

- Actionable dashboards, moderation-ready operations, and automation that drafts/suggests only.

## Phase I — SEO, performance, accessibility

- Schema and sitemap audits; noindex policy; browser/a11y/performance budgets.

## Phase J — release validation

- Migration, security, test, browser, media, schema, backup/restore, artifact, and deployment quality gates.

### Ordering rule

P0 trust/quality defects block P1 UX work when they affect a public claim. Every phase needs scope, changes, migration assessment, tests, screenshots/QA evidence, risks, rollback, and explicit approval.
