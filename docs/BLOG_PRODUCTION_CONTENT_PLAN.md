# Blog Production Content Plan

The production seed contains twenty original Turkish editorial guides across
Kıbrıs Mutfağı, Ev Ölçüleri, Saklama Rehberleri, Pişirme Teknikleri, Beslenme
Bilgileri, Mevsimsel Tarifler and Mutfak Organizasyonu. Every published post
has a unique purpose, metadata, a canonical URL, at least two verified recipe
links, editorial approval, originality status and a review date.

Food-safety posts cite an official safety source in their private source notes
and present only general guidance. They are not medical advice. Content
automation may create briefs and drafts but `CONTENT_AUTO_PUBLISH=false` is a
non-negotiable default.

The supplied covers are unique branded editorial fallbacks. They are visible
because they make the page layout usable, but their status remains explicit
until an editor reviews an approved licensed hero image.
